## Objective
Redesign the History screen to show medication adherence over time. Users should understand their patterns and feel motivated by their progress.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Horizontal week calendar strip at top (swipeable)
- [ ] Selected day shows detailed log below
- [ ] Stats cards: Adherence %, Current streak, Best streak
- [ ] Weekly adherence chart (simple bar chart)
- [ ] Day log shows all doses with timestamps and status
- [ ] Color-coded status indicators (taken=green, skipped=gray, missed=red)
- [ ] Date picker for jumping to specific date
- [ ] Empty state for days with no medications
- [ ] Smooth transitions when changing dates
- [ ] All icons are SF Symbols

### Week Strip Component
- Horizontal ScrollView with snap behavior
- Today highlighted
- Dots indicate dose completion for that day

### Adherence Chart
- Simple bar chart (can use react-native-svg or just Views)
- Each bar = day's adherence %
- Teal for high, amber for medium, red for low
